# Validatopia brand

- **Palette:** navy `#0A1B37` and green `#4EBC7D` on white, with teal `#0E7C7B` for card art.
  Light mode leads with navy; dark mode is navy backgrounds with green as the primary colour.
- **Contrast:** every text/fill pair used by the apps meets WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for
  UI). Green never carries text or icons on white; `--vt-color-success` (#1B7A45) is the green
  for text on light backgrounds.
- **Status:** trust badges always pair an icon and a word with their colour.
- **Type:** the platform's system font, on the Material 3 type scale.
- **Shapes:** rounded: cards, photos and camera views 16px; standard buttons fully rounded (pill);
  the wallet's large Share button 24px.
- **Card art:** a dark background with three see-through rolling hills, the document type as the
  title, the issuer or purpose under it, and a "Powered by" credit with the white VALID8
  Advisory logo. Colour pairs per document: Photo ID navy/green, Driver Licence teal/navy, Gym Membership
  navy/teal, Age Verification teal/green.
- **Logos:** `assets/valid8_advisory_logo.png` on light backgrounds,
  `assets/valid8_advisory_logo_white.png` on navy. `assets/epassport.svg` is the ICAO chip symbol
  for passport-related screens.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA throughout: 48px minimum touch targets, text that reflows at
  200% zoom, no hard timeouts.
